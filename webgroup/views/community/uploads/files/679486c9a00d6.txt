Admin users:
Have access to admin panel
Can change users
Can change role of users (admin or user)
Transfer community ownership
Remove accounts 



Regular users:
Can create community in any category
Join with any existing community
Update profile details
Delete their account 


Within a community users can:
Upload files to the community (notes/images/documents)
Search for files in search bar
Chat with other members



 Community admin can:
Remove users
Change community details
Delete files uploaded into the community
Delete the community 
Transfer admin to another user
